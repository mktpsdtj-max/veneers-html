What Are Ceramic Veneers?

A veneer is a layer of material placed over a tooth: an ultra-thin shell, custom-made for your mouth, bonded to the front of your natural teeth. Ceramic veneers improve the appearance of your smile and protect the tooth surface from damage, reflecting light just like natural enamel.

Veneers for stained teeth

They cover severe discoloration and help prevent the tooth from staining further over time.

Veneers for damaged teeth

They cover chips and cracks, and protect the tooth surface from future damage.

Veneers for an even smile

They correct worn teeth, gaps and mild visual misalignment, without years of braces.
